# How to log in to DEH Maintenance — Tenant Guide

This guide walks you through signing into your tenant account for the very first time, and also for every time after that. It's written assuming you've never used the system before.

If you ever get stuck, the last section ("If something goes wrong") tells you what to do.

> **📞 Office helpline: +91 810 721 6176** — call this number any time for help with login, your account, or anything in this app.

---

## What you'll need before you start

1. **Your phone or computer with internet.** Any modern browser works — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. You don't need to install anything.
2. **Your login ID.** This is two things joined by a dash:
   - The **building code** (a short 2–3 letter abbreviation for your building).
   - Your **flat number**.

   It looks like this: `BM-401` (Badri Mahal, flat 401), or `HM1-504` (Hashmat Manzil 1, flat 504).

   👉 If you don't know your building code, call the society office on **+91 810 721 6176**. The codes are also posted in the small **green tag** sometimes shown in the building lobby.

3. **A few minutes of quiet time** the first time you log in, because you'll be choosing your password and confirming your details. After this first session it takes only a few seconds each time.

---

## Step 1 — Open the website

Open your browser and go to:

> **https://deh-ticketing.vercel.app**

*(If your society shares a custom address later — like `tickets.dehmaintenance.com` — use that one instead. It works the same.)*

You'll see a **cream/beige patterned background** with a **white card in the centre of the screen**. At the top of the card is a small dark navy square with a yellow spanner icon, and the words **"DEH Maintenance"**.

Below that, inside the card, you'll see:
- A heading that says **"Welcome back"**
- Two buttons side-by-side: **Tenant** and **Staff**
- An input box (a thin rectangle to type into)

---

## Step 2 — Make sure the "Tenant" tab is selected

The two buttons at the top of the form work like tabs:
- **Tenant** ← that's you. Click here.
- **Staff** — this is for admin/manager/DR. Don't use this.

The selected tab turns **white** with bold text. The unselected one is gray.

If you accidentally click Staff, just click Tenant again. Nothing breaks.

---

## Step 3 — Type your login ID

Click inside the input box. It will be labeled **"Building & Flat"** with a small placeholder hint: *e.g. ABC-101*.

Type your login ID using these rules:
- **Building code** in letters (it doesn't matter if you type uppercase or lowercase — the system handles both).
- A **dash** (`-`) — the regular hyphen key, right next to the `0` key on most keyboards.
- **Your flat number** (digits, or sometimes digits + a letter like `10A`).

✅ Examples that work:
- `BM-401`
- `bm-401` (lowercase is fine)
- `HM1-504`
- `7CR-302`

❌ Examples that won't work:
- `BM 401` (space instead of dash — needs a dash)
- `BM_401` (underscore instead of dash)
- `BM/401` (slash instead of dash)
- `Badri Mahal 401` (the full building name — use the **code** instead)

---

## Step 4 — Click the "Continue" button

Underneath the input box you'll see a **big blue rounded button** that says **Continue** with a small arrow `→` on the right.

Click it.

The button will briefly say **"Checking…"** while the system looks you up.

**What happens next depends on whether this is your first time logging in.** Skip ahead:
- 👉 **First time ever?** Go to [Step 5 — First-time login](#step-5--first-time-login-only-the-very-first-time).
- 👉 **You've used the app before?** Go to [Step 6 — Returning login](#step-6--returning-login-every-time-after-the-first-time).

---

## Step 5 — First-time login (only the very first time)

If you've never used the system before, the screen will change automatically. Instead of a password box, you'll be taken to a page called **"Choose your password"**.

You'll see two input boxes:

1. **New password** — type a password that **only you know**. It must be at least **6 characters**. You can use letters, numbers, and symbols. Mix them for safety (e.g. `Saiful#22` or `bluedoor7!`).
2. **Confirm password** — type the **same password again**. This is to make sure you didn't make a typo.

⚠️ **Important — don't use the default password `1234`.** That's a temporary one and the system makes you change it.

Click the blue **Save and continue** button.

If the two passwords don't match, you'll see a red note. Fix it and try again.

### After your password is saved

You'll be sent to a screen called **"Confirm your details"**. This is a one-time check to make sure we have your correct information.

You'll be asked to fill in or verify:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Your name as you'd like it shown on tickets. Pre-filled if we already have it. |
| **Phone number** | Your 10-digit mobile number (e.g. `9876543210`). Don't add `+91` — the system handles that. |
| **Email address** | Your **real** email — Gmail, Yahoo, work email, whatever you use. **Do not** use anything ending in `@tenants.local` (that's a placeholder the system gave you). |
| **Building** | Already shown — verify it's correct. If wrong, call the society office on **+91 810 721 6176**. |
| **Wing** | The wing letter or word (A, B, North, etc.). |
| **Flat** | Your flat number. |
| **Address note** *(optional)* | Anything extra — e.g. "ring the doorbell twice" or "use the side gate". |

Click **Save and continue**.

You're now in! You'll land on your **Dashboard** — see [Step 7 — What you'll see after logging in](#step-7--what-youll-see-after-logging-in).

---

## Step 6 — Returning login (every time after the first time)

If you've logged in before, after pressing Continue you'll see the password input appear underneath. Above it there's a small blue strip that confirms **"Continuing as BM-401"** (or whatever your login was). Next to it is a small **"Change"** link if you typed the wrong ID — click that to go back.

In the **Password** field, type the password **you chose during your first login**.

Click the blue **Sign in** button.

That's it — you're in. Continue to [Step 7](#step-7--what-youll-see-after-logging-in).

---

## Step 7 — What you'll see after logging in

You'll land on your **Dashboard**. Here's what's on it, top to bottom:

### 🟦 Header bar (always at the top)
A dark navy bar with:
- **Yellow spanner icon + "DEH Maintenance"** on the left, with the word **"TENANT"** in small letters below it.
- **Dashboard** and **Raise ticket** links in the middle.
- A small circle with your initials, a **Password** link, and a **Log out** button on the far right.

### 🟨 Greeting
- A short label saying **GOOD MORNING / GOOD AFTERNOON / GOOD EVENING** depending on the time of day.
- A big bold line: **"Hello, [your full name]"**.
- A grey line below showing your building, wing, and flat.

### 🟧 "Your details" card
A white card showing your Name, Contact (phone), Location (building), Wing, and Flat. A small pill on the right tells you if your details are:
- **"auto-filled"** — never edited.
- **"edited by tenant"** — you've made changes.

To change anything: click **"Edit details"** on the right of this card. The fields turn into typeable boxes. Make your changes, then click **Save**. (Click **Cancel** to abandon changes.)

### 🟥 "My tickets" section
This is where every issue you've ever raised will be listed.

- **If you have no tickets yet:** you'll see a polite empty state with a "Raise one now →" link.
- **If you have tickets:** they appear as a list. Each row shows:
  - A small **coloured dot** (red = Open, blue = In Progress, green = Resolved)
  - The **issue type** (e.g. "Water leak", "AC not working")
  - A small **📎** with a number if you attached any photos
  - The **ticket number** (e.g. `TK-00123`)
  - **When** you submitted it
  - A coloured pill on the right showing the status

Click any row to see the full ticket — description, photos, current status.

To raise a new ticket, click the blue **"Raise a ticket"** button in the top right of this section, or the **"Raise ticket"** link in the header bar.

## If something goes wrong

### "No tenant matches that building/flat."

The most common reason is a typo.

**Check:**
1. Is your building code right? Ask the office or check the lobby board.
2. Did you use a **dash** (`-`), not a space or slash?
3. Did you spell your flat number the way the office has it on record? (`10A`, not `10 A`. `B-301`, not `B 301`.)

If it still doesn't work after these checks, call the society office on **+91 810 721 6176**. We may have your flat under a different label or you may belong to a building we haven't added yet.

### "Invalid login. Check your details and try again."

This happens on the password screen if the password is wrong.

**Try:**
1. Make sure **Caps Lock** is OFF.
2. Make sure you're typing the password you set up — not your email password or your phone unlock code.
3. If you've truly forgotten it, call the society office on **+91 810 721 6176**. The admin can reset it. You'll get a temporary password (`1234`) and will be asked to choose a new one on your next login.

### The page won't load / spins forever

1. Refresh the browser tab (press `F5` or pull down on mobile).
2. Try a different browser if you can (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
3. Check your internet connection.
4. If still broken, call the society office on **+91 810 721 6176** and mention what you saw — they'll let the IT team know.

### I see a screen I don't recognise / I'm stuck on a form

If you get stuck on the **"Choose your password"** or **"Confirm your details"** screens, **do not close the browser yet** — close it only after you've clicked **Save and continue** at least once. That's what finishes the first-time setup.

If you do close it accidentally, just go back to https://deh-ticketing.vercel.app and start over. Your earlier inputs are not lost.

### I accidentally clicked "Log out" / I want to log back in

No problem. Just go to https://deh-ticketing.vercel.app and log in again. Your tickets and details are saved.

## Tips for a smooth experience

- **Bookmark the login page** in your browser so you don't have to type the URL every time.
- **Don't share your password.** Each tenant has their own login. If your spouse also wants to raise tickets, ask the office to set up a separate login for them.
- **Use a real email address** during the first-time profile confirmation. We may use it later to send you ticket updates.
- **Add a clear photo** when raising a ticket about something visual (leak, crack, broken fitting). It speeds up the response.
- **Sessions expire after about an hour of inactivity** — perfectly normal. Just log in again.
